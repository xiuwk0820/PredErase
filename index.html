PredErase

Training-Free Object-and-Effect Removal with Predictive Latent Guidance.

PaperCode

Erase the object—and its effects

PredErase removes an object together with residual cast shadows and contact shading. It keeps FLUX.2-klein-4B and I-JEPA frozen, steering only latent representations at test time.

Contact-aware support

A geometric prior expands the instance mask to cover visual effects.

Predictive guidance

Cached I-JEPA hole predictions guide sparse updates inside editable regions.

Frozen models

No paired training or weight updates are required for inference.

Method

Evaluation

Evaluated on RemovalBench, RORD-Val, and DEFACTO-Val using OmniEraser and SmartEraser protocols.
